## 캡션 (최종본)

```
Most Dubai renovation problems start before any work begins — with a permit that was never filed.

Painting and flooring only need your building's NOC. But the moment a wall moves, or plumbing, AC or electrical changes, you need an authority permit — Dubai Municipality for most of the city, Trakhees on the Palm, DDA in certain districts.

Here's the part that catches people out: you cannot apply for the permit yourself. Only a contractor whose trade licence covers the work can file it. A firm without the right licence either works without a permit or borrows someone else's — and both leave you holding the risk.
```
