### A-3. Reddit r/dubai (밸류 퍼스트 — 링크는 댓글 요청 올 때만)

제목: **What I learned about renovation contracts in Dubai (after helping 3 friends who got burned)**

> A few things that would have saved my friends thousands of dirhams:
>
> 1. Never pay more than 20-30% upfront. The standard structure is 5 payments tied to milestones, with 5-10% held back until snagging is done.
> 2. No written variation order = no obligation to pay. Verbal "extras" agreed on site don't count.
> 3. Since January, Dubai's Law No. 7 of 2025 requires every contractor to be in a unified DM register — you can verify any company's licence on Invest in Dubai before paying a fils.
> 4. If a contractor takes your deposit and disappears, that's criminal fraud — Dubai Police 901, not just a consumer complaint.
> 5. DET consumer complaints (600 545 555 / consumerrights.ae) are free and actually work if you have a paper trail.
>
> Happy to answer questions. (I run a free contractor-comparison site for Dubai — link in comments if anyone wants it, not trying to spam the sub.)
